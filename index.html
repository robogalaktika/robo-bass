Patterns are written as scale degrees (1, 3, 5…), not fixed notes. Press a key: if stopped, the pattern starts playing in that key; if playing, it moves to that key from the next note. Press STOP to stop. Top row is major, bottom row is minor (3 and 6 drop a semitone).
Tap a cell: hit → accent → clear. The HOLD row sustains a note into the next step.
Phone speakers can't reproduce low bass clearly: use headphones or an amp, or raise the octave while practising.
